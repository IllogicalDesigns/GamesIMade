"Cool game! I love the particle effects on the vacuum, it makes it clear what its purpose is. The recharge sound effect was pretty funny too. Nice job!" - recurve-games

The vacuum didn't seem very reliable, sometimes I felt I was on target and it didn't scoop them. However, it was fun finding the ghosts using the radio ping and chasing them around was entertaining too." - fuseinabowl
