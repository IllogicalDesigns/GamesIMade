Description

You are a mouse, small and swift. But in this house, you are prey. Hungry predators lurk in every shadow, watching your every move. Scavenge for food to survive, but beware: one wrong step could be your last. Collect food, or starve... or die trying.

Goal: Collect food for your family of mice and escape alive!

Controls:
WASD to move
Space to jump/double jump
Mouse to aim
Left mouse button to throw noisemakers
Left control or c to sneak
Q or E to lean character
F for Gouda Positioning Service (GPS)
