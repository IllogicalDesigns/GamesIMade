Description

You are Santa and your elves have been turned evil by Chthulhu. They have shrunken you down, and themselves, and are out to kill you. Jump into a wacky world to fight Santa's evil elves with only Christmas force. Yes, that is right Santa uses the force, and you can too. Use that force to kill the elves and avoid an untimely death yourself.
