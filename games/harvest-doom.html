Description

Try your hand at farming after an eternity in hell. However, farming is not quite as easy as you expected when a new villian comes to your farm. Withstand the onslaught from Monsatan as they try to take over your farm. Planting their crops to claim its their land, don't just stand there. Grab your shotgun and get to farming.

Warning, the game has no end state and will continue forever. :(
