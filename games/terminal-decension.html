Description

Take a dive into the world of extreme mining. Grab your gun and jump, dodge, and shoot your way to victory.

Well avoiding the falls during your free fall, you will be tasked with collecting as much gold as possible. The only logical way to do this is grab your favorite type of gun and start shooting everything. Be warned though some miner setup traps to protect their shafts from other miners.

The game does not have an end and will proceed to loop after 5 levels. Also sometimes when switching guns they will run into a bug where they permanetly stop firing, the only fix is to restart.

WASD to move
Left mouse to fire
123 to switch weapons once purchased
Hold shift to deploy the 1 time use parachute to slow down
