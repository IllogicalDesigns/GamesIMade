Description

The backstory is that you are getting food for your child. With nothing but the shirt on your back you head out to get food for them. Of course this means the local thugs come after you. So be prepared to fight them off. The game is endless as I didn't have enough time to complete that portion. More platforms coming soon, maybe a post jam version.
