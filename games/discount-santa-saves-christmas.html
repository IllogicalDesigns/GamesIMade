Description

Discount Santa Saves Christamas is a platformer with a twisted Christmas styling. The game has three levels and is mostly working, focusing on Santa navigating a deadly obstacle course. While the original submission notes that the web client was broken, the core game mechanics are praised for being fiendishly difficult yet worth persevering with, featuring very well-balanced platforming.
